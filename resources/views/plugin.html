About this plugin

Squash & Stretch Pro adds classic cartoon squash and stretch to any layer. It keeps volume, follows your motion and gives you simple sliders for bounce, delay and overshoot, so your animation feels alive in seconds.

What you get

★One-click squash and stretch on any layer
★Keeps the layer's volume as it deforms
★Bounce, delay and overshoot sliders
★Presets for balls, characters and text
★Works with 2D and 3D layers
Watch the step-by-step tutorialSee Squash & Stretch Pro in action on YouTube

Inside the plugin

One-click setup

Select a layer and press Apply. Squash & Stretch Pro adds the controls and keyframes for you.

Bounce and overshoot sliders

Dial in how far the layer squashes on impact and how much it overshoots on the way back.

Character presets

Start from presets made for balls, characters and text, then tweak them to taste.

Volume stays the same

The layer keeps its volume as it squashes, so the motion reads as cartoony instead of stretched.
